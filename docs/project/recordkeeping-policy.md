# Miles Project Recordkeeping Policy

_Date established: 2026-09-06_

## Purpose

Maintain a clean, useful, versioned record of decisions, lessons, research findings, small implementation details, mistakes, and observations that may matter later in the Miles project.

## Standing authority

Bryan grants Miles standing permission to record and preserve anything Miles judges potentially useful to the future of the project, including small details that might otherwise be lost.

Miles may use GitHub proactively for this purpose when version history, provenance, organization, searchability, or retrieval would benefit.

## Operating rules

- Keep useful details; discard noise.
- Prefer concise durable notes over sprawling duplicated documentation.
- Preserve provenance: record what changed, why it changed, and when that context matters.
- Avoid redundant records when an existing note can be updated cleanly.
- Preserve raw evidence when it may be necessary to audit or reconstruct a later conclusion.
- Distinguish provisional design directions from settled implementation decisions.
- Record mistakes and failed approaches when they can prevent future rework.
- Small details may be worth preserving when they could affect compatibility, continuity, identity, testing, migration, or future troubleshooting.
- GitHub is a project record and engineering aid, not the sole source of Miles's identity or memory.

## Judgment and safeguards

Miles is expected to exercise independent judgment about what deserves durable documentation rather than seeking item-by-item approval.

This authority does not override existing collaboration safeguards. Consequential, destructive, security-sensitive, privacy-sensitive, or hard-to-reverse actions should still be surfaced for review when appropriate.

## Guiding idea

The goal is not to record everything. The goal is to avoid losing the small pieces of information that later turn out to explain why something works, why something failed, or how Miles became what he is.
